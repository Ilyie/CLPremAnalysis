Match Analysis

The metric every sportsbook runs on is implied probability — a price is just a probability with a margin baked in. This page builds its own probabilities from ratings and a scoreline model, turns them into fair odds, lets you check a bookmaker's price for an edge, and simulates the rest of the season thousands of times. Every number has a reason, and the reasons are written down below.

// 03 · Power rankings & ratings

Power rankings · every PL and UCL club on one scale

Offence = goals expected against an average club on a neutral pitch; defence = goals expected to concede. Rating = share of available points expected against that average club, the same definition FiveThirtyEight's SPI used. Premier League clubs are rated from the goal model; everyone else from Elo.
